Good to know

A question
before your trip?

The key answers for your holiday apartment stay in Dinkelsbühl.

How many guests can stay in the apartments?+

The AltstadtApartment sleeps up to 4 guests. The DesignApartment sleeps up to 6 guests. The apartments are booked separately.

Is Wi-Fi and a private kitchen available?+

Yes. Both apartments include free Wi-Fi and a fully equipped kitchen.

Where can I park?+

According to the host, free parking is available around a five-minute walk away. Please ask before arrival for parking and access details.

What are the check-in and check-out times?+

Check-in is generally between 3:00 pm and 6:00 pm, and check-out is by 10:00 am unless otherwise agreed.

Are pets allowed?+

Pets are only allowed by prior agreement with the host. Please clarify this before booking. Smoking is not permitted in the apartments.

Where can I see available dates and prices?+

Use “Plan your stay” to choose an apartment and open the existing Smoobu booking system, where you can check availability and the conditions for your stay.
