ADVENT IN THE SPITALHOF

23.11. – 22.12.2026

Christmas market

Spitalhof · historic old town

Golden lights. Mulled wine. Christmas joy.

As the lights come on in the Spitalhof, Dinkelsbühl welcomes its cosiest season. Festively decorated stalls offer mulled wine, regional treats and little discoveries, surrounded by the historic old town.

Combine your visit with the arts and crafts market in the concert hall and a stroll through the festive streets. After dark, the warm lights make the setting especially atmospheric.

Opening hours
Mon–Fri 1–8 pm · Sat–Sun 11 am–8 pm
Discover
Arts and crafts, gifts & regional specialities

Our tip: Visit in the late afternoon, then enjoy a relaxed evening in the old town.

HISTORY COMES TO LIFE

16.07. – 25.07.2027

Kinderzeche

Old town & festival grounds in Dinkelsbühl

A whole town celebrates its history.

Historic costumes, music and festive processions: Kinderzeche is part of Dinkelsbühl’s identity. Its historical play tells the traditional story of Kinderlore and the children said to have saved the town from destruction during the Thirty Years’ War.

The celebrations include the historical play, the ceremonial handover of the town, the procession and the fair. Individual performances have their own schedules and may require admission tickets.

Experience
Historical play, procession & fair
Planning
Check performance times and tickets with the organiser

Our tip: Make time for a procession and the historical play, with the old town as their memorable backdrop.

LOUD MUSIC. GREAT MOMENTS.

18.08. – 21.08.2027

Summer Breeze Open Air

Festival grounds near Dinkelsbühl

Festival energy meets old-town comfort.

For four days, the Dinkelsbühl region becomes a meeting place for metal fans. Summer Breeze Open Air combines international bands, multiple stages and the atmosphere of a major summer festival.

The festival takes place outside the old town. Plan your journey to the grounds separately and check the organiser’s latest travel advice. After a full festival day, an apartment offers your own place to unwind.
